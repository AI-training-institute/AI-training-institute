Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses and internship programs.

General Information

1. Can I apply directly for an internship without enrolling in a course?

Yes, you can apply directly for our internship programs without enrolling in a course.

2. Can I do an internship after completing a course?

Yes, students who complete our courses are eligible to apply for internships.

3. Will I receive a certificate after completing the internship?

Yes, all interns will receive an official Internship Completion Certificate upon successful completion.

4. Do you provide a certificate after course completion?

Yes, students will receive a Course Completion Certificate after successfully finishing their course.

5. Are physical classes available, or is it only online?

We offer both options. You can choose between physical (on-campus) classes and online learning.

6. Is it necessary to pay the registration fee in advance?

Yes, the registration fee must be paid in advance to confirm your enrollment.

7. Will I receive proper support during the course or internship?

Yes, we provide continuous mentorship, guidance, and technical support throughout your learning journey.

8. What type of learning approach do you follow?

We focus on project-based, practical learning to ensure real-world skills development.

Advanced FAQs

9. What is the duration of your courses and internships?

Course durations vary depending on the program, while internships typically last between 1 to 2 months.

10. What are the eligibility requirements to enroll?

There are no strict eligibility criteria. Anyone with basic knowledge and a willingness to learn can enroll.

11. Do you offer job placement after course or internship completion?

We do not guarantee job placement, but we provide career guidance, portfolio building, and interview preparation support to help you secure opportunities.

12. Is there a refund policy if I change my mind?

Registration fees are generally non-refundable. However, in special cases, requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

13. Can I switch from online to physical classes (or vice versa)?

Yes, you may request to switch modes depending on availability and scheduling.

14. Will I work on real projects during the course or internship?

Yes, our programs include real-world projects to give you practical exposure and hands-on experience.

15. What kind of support will I receive after completing the program?

You will continue to receive limited post-completion support, including guidance on projects, career advice, and learning resources.

16. Are the classes beginner-friendly?

Yes, our courses are designed to accommodate beginners as well as intermediate learners.

17. How can I register for a course or internship?

You can register through our website by filling out the registration form and completing the payment process.
